Description

Chen (橙) is a Nekomata and Ran Yakumo's shikigami. Chen first appeared in Perfect Cherry Blossom as the midboss and boss of Stage 2 and also as the extra stage midboss as well as Ran Yakumo's familiar in one of her spell cards.

Use of sorcery (while possessed as shikigami)

In shikigami state, Chen becomes extremely dangerous and is able to use various kinds of sorcery. She uses techniques that focus on speed and precise, tricky movements to confuse her opponents, making it very similar to sleight of hand. For example, she could make it appear as if she is dashing to the left, but will then attack from the right instead, moving so quickly that to humans it appears like instant teleportation.[4]


Surprising people (as bakeneko)

When she becomes a regular bakeneko, she becomes more feral and will resort to more physical attacks like biting and clawing with her long nails. Rather than saying she possesses a special ability to surprise accompanying humans, it is more strongly an implication that she's not able to do anything other than things of this extent. (However, even so, for ordinary humans it is dangerous enough). This is the same ability that Kogasa Tatara has - thus in this aspect, they have mutuality.


Shikigami powers

As a Shikigami, Chen is able to process large amounts of data in a instant. Chen used this ability to find an outlier Phantom among a large group of Phantoms by analyzing their movement patterns and difference in spiritual power.[5] However, unlike Ran, Chen is said to be bad with numbers.[6]
