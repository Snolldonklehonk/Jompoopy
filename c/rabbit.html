Description

Reisen Udongein Inaba (鈴仙・優曇華院・イナバ) is a former Moon rabbit that serves as Eirin Yagokoro's student and medicine peddler, residing in Eientei after fleeing from the Moon. She first appeared as the stage 5 boss of Imperishable Night. Since Phantasmagoria of Flower View, she has appeared as a playable character in multiple games including Legacy of Lunatic Kingdom.


Fun Fact:


Although sharing a last name with Tewi, they aren't actually related. As Reisen is a Lunarian Rabbit

Abilities


Manipulation of insanity and wavelengths



Reisen has the ability to sense and manipulate waves of all kinds - including waves of light, sound, space, the waves that make up all things, and the waves that make up the thoughts and feelings of living creatures. Her signature use of this ability is manipulating a creature's waves to induce insanity. Reducing the wavelength of brainwaves make her target short-tempered and irrational, while increasing the wavelength leave her target apathetic and depressed. Increasing the amplitude of a wave results in an "excess" of existence, which she can use to transmit her thoughts no matter how far, while reducing the amplitude results in a "rarefaction" of existence, which she can use to silence a voice. Synchronizing waves removes interference, resulting in intangibility; offsetting the phases negates existence, which can completely hide a subject's form.[10] Reisen can also manipulate light and sound waves to deceive the senses, such as by creating hallucinations, concealing her danmaku, and tuning the vision of her enemy. She is also able to create illusions of herself that can attack the enemy, and can cast wide-area illusions that cause people to get lost.

Reisen's red eyes have a similar effect as the true full moon and can induce insanity. She can use this ability to disorient and dizzy a target, preventing them from moving straight. In addition, she is capable of releasing waves of madness from her eyes in ripples or in destructive beams.[11]

Reisen is immune to the abilities of the Three Fairies of Light (seeing an invisible Sunny Milk, hearing a silent Luna Child and escaping Star Sapphire's detection) since they are based on waves. However, she seems unable to replicate Star Sapphire's abilities with her own.[12]


Moon rabbit abilities

Moon rabbits can pick up "ESP waves" with their ears, forming a sort of psychic network where they can detect feelings and rumors from other moon rabbits, no matter how far they are. Reisen can still participate in this network despite the physical and spiritual distance between Gensokyo and the Moon.
