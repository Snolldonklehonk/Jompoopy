Description

Zanmu Nippaku (日白 残無 Nippaku Zanmu) is a Human-Oni, or a human who became an oni, who gained great influence in Hell after relocating from the Former Hell to the current Hell. Zanmu Nippaku was formerly a human monk during the Sengoku Jidai period before she started absorbing beast-youkai spirits. Sensing her imminent fall to Hell, Zanmu ventured there prematurely then transformed into an oni who ruled (Former) Hell being admired for her strength, intelligence and charisma. Zanmu first appeared as the main antagonist in Unfinished Dream of All Living Ghost as a possible opponent as well as a playable character.

Abilities


Manipulating nothingness



Zanmu has the ability to manipulate nothingness. This causes the victim to lose their desires and motivations. It can be subtle, such as distracting the victim, or outright causing them to lose their will to fight or will to continue investigating. In Unfinished Dream of All Living Ghost, she uses her ability to distract Marisa Kirisame [4] and Sanae from the true cause of the incident. Then, she uses her ability to cause the beast spirits leaders to lose their will to fight. Finally, she uses her ability to convince Reimu to stop her investigation.

While Zanmu is able to manipulate nothingness of her victims, some appear to be less affected than others. Reimu's instincts continued to point to Zanmu as the true culprit and Reimu was not able to be manipulated to accept her plans for "resolving the incident". [5] Similarly, she was unable to stop Mamizou Futatsuiwa from continuing her investigating into the incident. [6] Also, due to Hisami's unpredictable masochism tendencies, Zanmu was not able to control Hisami the way she wanted.
