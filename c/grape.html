Description

SHisami Yomotsu (豫母都 日狭美 Yomotsu Hisami) is a Yomotsu-shikome, or underworld hag youkai, who is a subordinate to Zanmu Nippaku in Hell. Hisami is known to try and use her sweet words to drag people into Hell, using her ability to chase them if they run away without mattering how far they go. Hisami first appeared as a possible opponent, and playable character, in Unfinished Dream of All Living Ghost.

Abilities


Never letting anything slip from her grasp



Hisami can never let anything slip from her grasp. This ability aids her assignment of dragging people into Hell. If her target runs away, she will chase them to the ends of the earth and it's said that it's very likely that her target will fall into Hell the moment she sets her sights on them. However, she's said to be easily distracted by food such as grapes or bamboo shoots, which can be used as an opportunity to make a getaway.[4][5]
