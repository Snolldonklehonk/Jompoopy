Description

Aya Shameimaru (射命丸文 Shameimaru Aya) is a crow tengu capable of manipulating wind and serves as the journalist for the Bunbunmaru Newspaper writing articles, that are usually dubious, about anything in Gensokyo. She first appears in Bohemian Archive in Japanese Red as the reporter of its articles and has appeared as a playable character in multiple games since she was a playable character and possible opponent in Phantasmagoria of Flower View. She is also the main protagonist of Shoot the Bullet and Double Spoiler and a playable character in Hidden Star in Four Seasons while serving as the main reporter yet again in Alternative Facts in Eastern Utopia.

Abilities


Manipulation of wind



With this ability, Aya can create, control, and stop winds of incredible strength at will. This even extends to being able to create tornadoes. While her powers are among the top class in Gensokyo[2] and not many can really challenge her, she prefers to remain non-combative. She also rarely uses her powers just to show off, a characteristic that seems to be common amongst all tengu.[2][12]
