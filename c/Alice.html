Description

Alice Margatroid (アリス・マーガトロイド Arisu Māgatoroido) is a Youkai magician who can controls dolls and lives in a house within the Forest of Magic not far away from the home of Marisa Kirisame. She first appeared as the Stage 3 and Extra boss in the PC-98 game Mystic Square and then later reappeared in the Windows games as the midboss (twice) and boss of Stage 3 in Perfect Cherry Blossom. She became a playable character in multiple games since Imperishable Night, in a team along Marisa, and in Immaterial and Missing Power. She also is a possible supporting character for Marisa in Subterranean Animism.

Abilities


Magic, manipulating puppets/h3>

Magicians are a race that have their purpose of existence bound to their magical technique, and Alice's magic manifests itself with her enchanted dolls. [8] While her physical and magical ability are only average, [2] she is very dextrous [9] and is also able to manipulate a great amount of dolls at the same time as if they were living beings - a skillfulness which is among the finest in Gensokyo. [2] Her magic does not give any life to the dolls, but allows her to control them with magical threads, manipulating them by magic instead of using her hands. [1] She can make the dolls move in nearly any way a human could, and can even have them control other dolls. She mostly leaves cooking, laundry, cleaning, and other chores to her dolls, and as such usually has her hands full concentrating on commanding them. The one thing Alice does not have the dolls do is creating other dolls - she leaves this task to her hand only. [1].

When fighting, her army of dolls highly outnumber her opponents, and defeating some of them will just cause them to be replaced. [1] Many of her dolls are additionally filled with gunpowder [10] or can be detonated with magic power; once she is done using them, she typically repurposes them to act as kamikaze bombs, [9] a fact referenced in many of her spell cards (such as Magic Sign "Artful Sacrifice" and One Lemmings' Parade) and skills (such as "Doll Cremation" and "Ooedo Explosive Marionette"). [11] However, her weakness is that, as she is concentrating on manipulating the dolls, her movements are sluggish and her fighting abilities are low, leaving herself to be a prime target.[1] She usually leaves the firing of danmaku entirely to the dolls - in the rare cases where she joins in, her attacks are weaker and more plain.[9] [12]

Her dolls have been seen talking in some instances, such as Marisa and Alice's good ending in Subterranean Animism and in the prologue of Imperishable Night. Additionally, in chapter 5 of Strange and Bright Nature Deity, the dolls are seen with emotion, conversing back and forth with Alice, and even putting on snow clothing. However, Marisa points out that, as the dolls are not alive and are merely being manipulated to talk, it really is just a "lame one-person act".[13]

Alice's ultimate objective is to make a completely autonomous doll that have their own thoughts and move by their own volition. While she has poured her soul into making one, she still has not seen any success, and she admits that she has much to learn.[14] In order to perfect a completely autonomous doll, Alice does experiments relating to the link between the doll and the soul. One such experiment involved nailing humanoid straw dolls to trees - she stated that, as this causes the subject to feel pain as well, understanding this dynamic would allow her to better understand this link.[14] In Scarlet Weather Rhapsody, she states that string between the body and soul is the same link that allows her to manipulate dolls. [15] Despite the effort she has put into creating a completely autonomous doll, she admits that dolls that she controls herself are more useful than one that would think for themselves.[16]
