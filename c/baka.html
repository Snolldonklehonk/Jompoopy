Description

Cirno (チルノ Chiruno) is an ice fairy who usually resides in an igloo close to Misty Lake. She is known for causing pranks that involve freezing objects or even toads while also considering herself to be stronger than everyone else. Cirno first appeared as the Stage 2 boss of Embodiment of Scarlet Devil and reappeared as a playable character, in multiple games since Phantasmagoria of Flower View. She stars as the main protagonist of Great Fairy Wars and is one of several protagonists in Touhou Hisoutensoku as well as Hidden Star in Four Seasons.

Abilities

Manipulating cold

Cirno has the ability to manipulate cold to the point of being able to freeze water. It seems like her surroundings are chilly even in summer, as she emits cold air constantly.[5] In battle, she uses this ability to attack others with icicles, huge ice cubes, or wieldable weapons like swords, but also to freeze her opponents or their danmaku.[6][7] However, despite being able to freeze something hot like flaming danmaku, she is unable to freeze lasers.[8]

How she exactly manipulates cold is unclear, but Reisen Udongein Inaba speculates that when Cirno does it, she warms up inside as a result.[9] This would mean that she just absorbs the heat in the surroundings instead of "creating" cold from nothing.
