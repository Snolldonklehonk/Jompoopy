Description

Kaguya Houraisan (蓬莱山　輝夜 Houraisan Kaguya) is an immortal Lunarian who was once a princess of Lunar Capital on the Moon, before fleeing and residing in Eientei alongside Eirin Yagokoro. She first appears as the true Stage 6 boss of Imperishable Night, and subsequently appears in many print works, including prominently in Touhou Bougetsushou.


Fun Fact:


She and Mokou have a rivalry due to Kaguya killing Mokou's dad via an Impossible request that killed him.
