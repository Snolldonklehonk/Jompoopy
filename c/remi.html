Description

Remilia Scarlet (レミリア・スカーレット Remiria Sukāretto) is a vampire who is the head of the Scarlet Devil Mansion. She is the sister of Flandre Scarlet, the mistress of Hong Meiling and Sakuya Izayoi as well as the other fairy maids and the friend of Patchouli Knowledge. She first appears as the Stage 6 boss and main antagonist of the Embodiment of Scarlet Devil and has been a playable character in multiple games since Imperishable Night, in a team with Sakuya, and Immaterial and Missing Power as well.

Abilities as a vampire

Characteristically of vampires, Remilia has tremendous physical abilities and magical power, which according to Akyuu would allow one to uproot a thousand-year-old tree single-handedly and to run through the Human Village in a flash. While the true extent of these capabilities is unknown, Remilia's profile in Immaterial and Missing Power describes her as being "faster than the eye can follow, and strong enough to crush boulders".[1] While facing off against Watatsuki no Yorihime, she claims to have flown around the Moon in an instant.[11] Vampires also have the ability to summon and manipulate a great number of devils with a single shout, and to turn into a swarm of bats or even mist to escape or dodge attacks.[12] Additionally, in exchange for their growth and maturity, vampires like Remilia attained eternal life, and as long as their head remains intact they can regrow their entire body in just one night.[1][13] Because vampires possess all of these capabilities, they are among the strongest species in Gensokyo.[12]

However, like other vampires, her skin gets burnt when exposed to sunlight,[11] or when touching roasted soybeans.[12] She also can't pass through flowing water such as rain or rivers[14] and isn't able to approach sardine heads or broken holly branches.[12]


Manipulation of fate

Remilia claims to be able to manipulate fate,[15] but she was never explicitly shown activating her ability. It appears that those in Remilia's vicinity are followed by bad fortune, even if Remilia is unaware of said person.[3] While wondering if Remilia uses her ability consciously, Akyuu claims that Remilia can also bring great changes in one's daily life with nothing more than a word. Citing as an example being more likely to encounter rare things.[9]

According to her little sister Flandre Scarlet, the true nature of the ability may be to know what happens in the future, with the example of this ability being shown when she supposedly predicted beforehand the exact hour and day a meteor would fall to the Scarlet Devil Mansion. However, Flandre also said that Remilia "just likes to talk that way" and that it thus may just be an act of pretense.[16]
