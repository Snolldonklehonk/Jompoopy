Description

Hong Meiling (紅 美鈴 Hon Meirin) is a youkai who serves as the gatekeeper of the Scarlet Devil Mansion, and is known for her martial arts. She first appeared as the Stage 3 boss of Embodiment of Scarlet Devil and first became a playable character through a downloadable patch in Immaterial and Missing Power as a versus mode character with no story, as well as one of the three protagonists of Touhou Hisoutensoku.


Fun Fact:


She's Chinese

Abilities

Usage of Qi

Meiling is able to manipulate her life-breath, or qi. Her danmaku and her flight likely involve projecting her qi outward to attack foes or propel herself in a manner seen in martial arts folklore and in contemporary Japanese manga.
